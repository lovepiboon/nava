About Navatanee

A Legacy of Prestige & Precision

Navatanee Golf Course & Club stands as one of Thailand's most storied golfing institutions — a sanctuary where championship-caliber design meets refined hospitality.

Our History

Host to international championships, Navatanee has shaped the golfing heritage of the region for over five decades with its masterfully crafted layout.

Our Vision

To deliver an unrivalled golf lifestyle — blending world-class sport, elegant leisure, and a community of distinguished members.

  • Championship 18-hole layout
  • Award-winning clubhouse
  • Members-only privileges
  • Signature fine dining
